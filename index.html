Company Background

41+ Years of Technical Expertise

New FS Cool Aire Industries, Inc. is an independent company providing a comprehensive range of services aimed at the Air-Conditioning and Refrigeration Facilities Maintenance and Repair industry in the Philippines. It was formed to meet an identified need in the marketplace for office buildings, plants, and hotels to receive quality, "fit for purpose" engineering service at a reasonable cost.

What began in 1984 as Cool Aire Marketing in Makati has grown into a nationwide electro-mechanical contractor, with branches and alliances that let us handle large-scale projects across both government and private sectors.
