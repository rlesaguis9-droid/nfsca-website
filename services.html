What We Do

Our Services

From full mechanical installation to nationwide preventive maintenance, our team is organized along four disciplines: air-conditioning & refrigeration, ventilation, facilities management, and fabrication.

Technical Capabilities

Engineering & Installation, End to End

Every service below draws on 41+ years of hands-on work — installing, repairing, and maintaining systems for hotels, plants, retail buildings, banks, and government facilities across the Philippines.

Mechanical Works & Installation

Supply and installation of complete air-conditioning and refrigeration systems, sized and engineered for the building.

  • Water-cooled & air-cooled chillers
  • VRF / VRV systems
  • Air Handling Units (AHU)
  • Cooling towers & pump systems
  • Ductwork & air distribution

Electrical Works

Power and control systems that keep mechanical installations running safely and efficiently.

  • Electrical control panels
  • Motor control systems & VFD
  • Building Management Systems (BMS)
  • Cable trays & feeder lines
  • Power generator installation

Facilities Operation & Maintenance

Ongoing preventive maintenance programs, including multi-branch coverage for banks and hospitality groups.

  • Scheduled preventive maintenance
  • Multi-branch / nationwide coverage
  • Hotel & guest-room AC servicing
  • Equipment efficiency optimization

Fabrication Works

In-house fabrication of core HVAC components, built to spec for each project.

  • Air Handling Units (AHU)
  • Chilled water cooling coils
  • Heat exchangers & condensers
  • Fan Coil Units (FCU)

Repair & Overhauling

Restoring critical equipment to full working order rather than defaulting to costly replacement.

  • Compressor overhauling & rewinding
  • Chiller rehabilitation
  • Cooling tower filler replacement
  • Chemical descaling & cleaning

Duct Cleaning & Decontamination

Indoor air-quality service for plants and industrial facilities where ductwork accumulates dust and residue over time.

  • Main duct line cleaning
  • Dust-collection line service
  • Before / after air quality checks
